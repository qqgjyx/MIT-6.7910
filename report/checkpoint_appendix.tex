\section{Metrics}
\label{app:metrics}

The runs of Step 2 save checkpoints every 1000 steps, at 12 log-spaced steps, and at three consecutive steps
$t,t+1,t+2$ for 24 values of $t$ from 25 to 9998; the metrics are measured at 34 of them per run, the swaps of Step 3
at their final weights. \emph{Weights}: over all parameters, $\|\theta\|_2$, $\|\theta-\theta_0\|/\|\theta_0\|$ and
$\|\theta\|_\infty/\|\theta\|_2$; means over the 36 matrices $W$ of the blocks of the stable rank
$\|W\|_F^2/\|W\|_2^2$, the effective rank $\exp H(\sigma/\textstyle\sum\sigma)$ of the singular values $\sigma$, the
spectral norm and $\sigma_1/\sigma_{\rm median}$. \emph{Updates} $\Delta\theta_t=\theta_{t+1}-\theta_t$:
$\|\Delta\theta_t\|/\|\theta_t\|$, its cosine with $\Delta\theta_{t+1}$ and with the negative gradient $-g_t$ on step
$t$'s batch, and the stable and effective rank of its block matrices $\Delta W$. \emph{Curvature}, on 32 fixed
training sequences: $\lambda_{\max}(H)$ (20 Lanczos iterations, logged during training), $\operatorname{tr}H$ (10
Rademacher probes), $\operatorname{tr}H/\lambda_{\max}$, and the batch sharpness
$\mathbb{E}_B[g_B^\top H_Bg_B]/\mathbb{E}_B\|g_B\|^2$. \emph{Predictions}, on the 2{,}048 held-out positions: entropy,
mean top-1 probability, calibration error (15 bins), top-1 accuracy, loss on the targets in the top and the bottom
third of training-set frequency and their difference, JS to the unigram and to the bigram model of the training split,
and validation minus training loss. \emph{Activations}, on 32 held-out sequences: attention entropy and attention on
the first token (means over layers, heads and positions), fraction of MLP pre-activations above 0, RMS of the
residual stream after the last block, and effective rank of the centred final features.

\section{Compute}
\label{app:compute}

All runs used one NVIDIA GPU each on the MIT ORCD cluster. GPU-hours are those Slurm allocated to
each suite, shared out over the runs of the suite by their training time where runs of several suites shared a GPU.
Not counted: the offline measurements (Muon's spectral-norm sharpness, loss barriers, distances, the metrics of
\cref{app:metrics}), a few GPU-hours.
